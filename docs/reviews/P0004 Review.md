# P0004 Review

## Status

Accepted

---

## Summary

Accepted with two editorial revisions.

The proposal successfully defines a standard template for recording Open Questions.

The overall structure is clear, and the separation of responsibilities aligns well with the architecture of ReasonOS.

Editorial revisions are recommended only to clarify the intended purpose of two sections.

---

## Findings

### Purpose

Accepted.

The proposal clearly defines its responsibility as introducing a template for Open Question documents.

No revisions are required.

---

### Status

Accepted.

Using `Open` as the default status is appropriate.

Additional statuses may be introduced by future proposals if necessary.

---

### Question

Accepted.

The following principle should remain unchanged.

> The question should be concise, objective, and independent of any proposed solution.

This establishes an important distinction between questions and proposals.

---

### Background

Accepted.

Providing the context behind the question improves traceability and future understanding.

---

### Why It Matters

Accepted.

Documenting the significance of the question helps prioritize future discussions.

---

### Current Understanding

Accepted.

Recording the current understanding specific to the question provides useful context without replacing the repository-wide `Current Understanding.md`.

---

### Possible Directions

Accepted with editorial revision.

Clarify that this section records exploratory ideas rather than proposed decisions.

Suggested wording:

> Record possible approaches for future discussion.
> These are exploratory ideas, not proposed decisions.

---

### Resolution Criteria

Accepted.

Defining clear criteria for resolving the question helps determine when the question can be considered complete.

---

### Related Documents

Accepted.

This section appropriately connects the question to other repository artifacts.

No structural changes are required.

---

### Notes

Accepted with editorial revision.

Clarify that this section is intended only for information that does not naturally belong elsewhere.

Suggested wording:

> Record additional observations that do not belong in other sections.

---

## Decision

Accepted with two editorial revisions.

---

## Actions

- Update the Open Question template using the accepted editorial revisions.
- No changes are required to the overall template structure.
