# Open Question #0001

## Status

Open

---

## Question

Is "Current Understanding" the most appropriate name for its responsibilities?

---

## Background

Current Understanding has gradually evolved beyond a simple record of understanding.

It now serves as:

- the authoritative reference for the current design,
- the definition of accepted concepts,
- the definition of accepted principles,
- the baseline for future design decisions.

This raises the question of whether its current name still accurately reflects its responsibilities.

---

## Why It Matters

A concept's name should accurately represent its responsibility.

An inappropriate name may lead to misunderstandings and reduce the clarity of the architecture.

---

## Current Understanding

The current name remains "Current Understanding."

No alternative has been accepted.

---

## Possible Directions

- Keep the current name.
- Rename it.
- Split its responsibilities.
- Introduce another concept.

---

## Resolution Criteria

This question can be resolved when the responsibilities of the concept become stable and its name clearly represents those responsibilities.
