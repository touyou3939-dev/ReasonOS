## Purpose

Define the standard development process for evolving ReasonOS.

---

## Proposed Change

Create `docs/Development Process.md` with the following content.

### Purpose

- Define how ReasonOS evolves.
- Encourage careful reasoning, explicit discussion, and incremental improvement.

### Development Flow

```text
Observation
        ↓
Open Question
        ↓
Discussion
        ↓
Commit Proposal
        ↓
Review
        ↓
Accepted
        ↓
Commit
        ↓
Current Understanding
```

### Principles

1. Do not introduce new concepts prematurely.
2. Every concept must have a clear responsibility.
3. Open Questions are valuable and should be preserved until sufficient understanding is obtained.
4. Repository structure should evolve according to responsibilities.
5. Development is iterative through repeated observation, discussion, and refinement.

---

## Rationale

ReasonOS evolves by following an explicit development process rather than ad hoc design decisions.

Repository structure should reflect conceptual responsibilities rather than implementation convenience.

The proposed process captures the practices established during Sprint 1 and provides a foundation for future development.
